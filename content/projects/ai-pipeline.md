**SocratexAI** is my multipurpose multi-agent-compatible AI pipeline.

The idea is to keep AI involved through the whole engineering loop,<br>
not just use it as a glorified "implement this" button.

The workflow covers things like:

- problem clarification and verification
- note-building and context gathering
- borrowed > invented solution exploration
- planning and architecture around best practices
- implementation and code review
- looking for further improvements after the task is already "done"

Different agents can take different roles,<br>
while the human stays responsible for context, decisions and verification.

The long-term goal is broader orchestration:<br>
describe a problem, decompose it, select the right agents and tools,<br>
then build and run a pipeline around it.

It's basically my attempt at making AI collaboration systematic instead of ad-hoc.
