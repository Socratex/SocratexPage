**Riftbound Vanguard** - movement-heavy 2D action roguelike-metroidvania<br>
For now it's in VERY early stages of development.

it will have procedural exploration, fast combat and modular systems<br>it's designed for replayability without mindless repetition.

The goal is to make every run feel dynamic<br>without turning the game into repetitive noise:

- handcrafted mechanics and physics
- modular rule-based world and enemy generation
- reactive combat and systems
- support for AI-generated missions, dialogue and events

<br>I'm a game collector and I keep notes on almost every played game.<br>At some point, that naturally turned into: "Fine, I'll make my own."

So I'm building one around the things I value most in games:<br>playability, movement, combat, variety and systems that stay interesting.

It's also a proving ground for my AI pipeline.<br>Because I don't know much about game-dev or Unity.
